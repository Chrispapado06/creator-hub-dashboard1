## Refund Policy

**Made-to-order items.**
Because every piece is produced specifically for you, we're not able to offer refunds or exchanges for change-of-mind purchases. Please check the size chart carefully before ordering.

**What we will fix, always:**
- Wrong item received
- Misprints, defects, or damaged items on arrival
- Sizing errors caused by our production process (not by the size chart)

If any of the above applies, contact us at support@theicefall.com within 14 days of delivery with photos of the issue, and we'll arrange a reprint or refund at no cost to you.
