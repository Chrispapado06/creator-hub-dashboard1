# UNCVRD Build Plan — final

*Panel: 3 scoring lenses + 6 analysis lenses over a 25-page ops doc, cross-checked against the live repo. Five "known facts" in this repo were tested against real recorded data during this pass and three of them were wrong. Corrections are called out inline.*

---

## 1. What the operation actually needs

The document is not short of targets — it has ~40 numbers across four horizons. It is short of *denominators*: the $10,000-per-account guarantee (p12), the 30-swapped-fans-per-day promo math (p12), the $5,000-pending salary benchmark (p18) and the $3k/shift chatter bar (p9) all have **zero instrumentation**, while the one thing that *is* instrumented — response time — is enforced at 7 minutes against a doc that says 3. Meanwhile every people and money decision (commission, keep-or-cut a salary, who gets promo slots) is made from numbers the person being measured typed in themselves, or from one manager's memory.

The leverage is not new systems. Roughly 70% of the required data is **already being fetched every four minutes by a Railway service and then thrown away** — transaction amounts discarded in `recordSpend()`, mass-message records kept for 4 days in a local JSON file, downtime alerts fired to Discord and never persisted, feed/story counts printed nightly and compared to nothing. The correct first move is to stop discarding, add thresholds, and route to one queue.

Everything that requires a Google Sheet conversion, an Infloww integration, or displacing three people's weekly workflow goes later — not because it's low value (internal promo is the highest-value item in the doc) but because it is blocked on things nobody has confirmed.

## 2. TIER 1 — build now

### T1-A. Posting & Cadence SLA (merges candidates 6, 18, 7-corrected, and the measurable half of 9)

**Panel agreement:** all three lenses. Feasibility 9/8, ROI 8/8, Duplication 7/6. The single highest-certainty build in the set.

**⚠️ Correction that changes this build's scope — verified today.** The repo-wide claim that *"mass-messaging returns empty for every account because MMs go through Infloww"* (README:43-45, CONVERSATION_ARCHIVE.md:62, repeated in five lens reports) is **false and stale**. `shift-downtime-monitor/mm-eod.json` contains **549 mass-message records across 4 days** (2026-07-02 → 07-05: 142/150/150/107), pulled by `listMassMessages()` from `GET /{acct}/mass-messaging/overview` (of.mjs:156) and written by `monitor.mjs:396-401`. Each record carries `{acct, date, sent, viewed, free, media, price, text}` — e.g. `["42813839820", {"acct":"Rose white","date":"2026-07-03T23:19:12+00:00","sent":117,"viewed":3,"free":true,"media":0,"price":0,"text":"thought you'd say \"hi\" at least 😣 x"}]`. **MM cadence (p8 #5, "every 30 mins – 1 hour") is directly computable today with zero inference.** Retire the stale claim in the README and archive before anyone re-derives it.

**Feature list**
1. `posting_targets` table per account: `stories_per_day`, `feed_posts_per_day`, `min_scheduled_queue`, `max_feed_gap_minutes`, `max_mm_gap_minutes`, `effective_from`. **Targets seed from each account's own trailing-14-day median plus a step-up**, not from the doc's numbers (see failure mode).
2. `daily_posting_counts` in Supabase — replaces the 4-day retention in `loadEod()` (`monitor.mjs:412`, `keep = [0,1,2,3]`), so week-over-week trend survives. Write MM counts and per-MM records alongside.
3. Verdict column on the existing nightly `buildAdminReport()` table (monitor.mjs:491): red/amber/green per account, **leading with the accounts that got worse**, passing accounts collapsed to one line.
4. **Intra-day gap alerts** — feed posts carry `postedAt`, so alert during waking hours when an account exceeds its feed gap; and **MM gap alerts during shift** (>90 min with no MM sent → ping the chatter in the shift channel, while the shift is still running).
5. Queue-depth floor on `countScheduledPosts()` (of.mjs:183) — today it only warns at *literally zero*.

**Reuses:** `recordEodActivity()` (monitor.mjs:393), `buildAdminReport()` (:491), `maybeSendEod()`, the EOD config block, `listFeedPosts`/`listStories`/`countScheduledPosts`/`listMassMessages` (of.mjs), the EOD_ADMIN_WEBHOOK, the record-by-id trick so deleted posts still count.
**New:** two Supabase tables, a threshold config, a verdict renderer, three gap checks. No new OF calls at all.

**Effort: 4 days** (3 for posting, +1 for MM cadence now that the data is confirmed present).

**Causal chain:** feed/story/MM volume is the promo inventory that produces the swapped fans the entire +$50,000/30-45-day plan (p12) is priced off. An account posting 1 story instead of 5 runs at 20% of traffic capacity, silently, daily. The MM gap alert is the shorter chain: p9 names MM→conversation→script the No.1 chatter skill, and a 3-hour MM gap is a dead shift — catching it at 90 minutes recovers *that shift's* revenue.

**The one thing that kills it:** setting the doc's targets literally. Observed reality on 2026-07-04 was **21 feed posts and 14 stories across all seven accounts combined** (~3 feed, ~2 stories per account) against targets of 12 and 5 *per account*. A static target makes every account red on night one and the report becomes wallpaper within a week — exactly what happened to the current all-numbers table it replaces. Median-seeded targets with a step-up, and "show only what got worse", are non-negotiable v1 scope. Also state on the report that this measures *volume*, not the p25 model→SFS→model rotation — `listFeedPosts` returns only `{id, date}` (of.mjs:173), so rotation is unverifiable.

### T1-B. Fan Spend Ledger + Whale Coverage (merges candidates 1, 11, 17, 25 — ship 17 + 25)

**Panel agreement:** all three lenses named this a single system and all three picked **[17]** as the base specification. Feasibility 8, ROI 8, Duplication 8.

**Feature list**
1. **Stop discarding transaction amounts.** `recordSpend()` (monitor.mjs:214-222) currently stores a bare ISO date per fanId and throws away the `amount` that `listTransactions()` (of.mjs:234-255) already parsed. Write every transaction to `fan_spend_events` (of_account_id, fan_id, fan_username, txn_id UNIQUE, amount, txn_type, occurred_at). ~20 lines plus pagination past `limit: 20`.
2. **Backfill from `lastspend.json`.** Verified structure: `{byAccount: {acct_id: {fanId: ISO-date}}}` — 3,896 fans across 8 accounts, earliest 2026-06-11. **Dates only, no amounts.** So it seeds `first_seen`, `last_spend` and the 10/21/45-day silence bands *from day one*; the rolling-30-day *dollar* figure still needs ~30 days to warm up. Say so up front.
3. **Derived rolling-30d spend** per fan + auto-flag at the doc's real **$3,000/month** whale line (p8 #17), sitting *alongside* the manual OF lists, never replacing them.
4. **Decay alert:** a $3k+ fan whose rolling 30d drops >60% vs prior 30d, or silent for 10/21/45 days → card to `postQaPins()` with the QA on shift @-mentioned, idempotent per band via `claim()`.
5. **Morning-untouched sweep (from candidate 25):** at a cutoff hour, for every whale, was there a creator-sent message since local midnight? Free where `/chats` lastMessage is already creator-sent; one `listChatMessages` call otherwise. Post the **miss list**. This inverts today's purely reactive whale card — which fires only when a whale is *active*, meaning the silent whale generates no card at all. Implements p8 #15 verbatim, and needs **no warm-up**, so it delivers value while the ledger accumulates.
6. **DO NOT SELL violation ping** — `whales.cardByAccount` already carries handling tags; fire when a PPV purchase lands on a DO-NOT-SELL fan.

**Reuses:** `sweepTransactions()` (monitor.mjs:245), `recordSpend()`, `sweepInactivity()` (7/14/28-day bands are already half the decay detector), `whaleRecentScript()` (:544, already does exactly the touch-check fetch under a claim gate), `claim()`/state.json, `postQaPins()`, `whales.json` 12h cache, the `WHALE_SUPABASE_*` service-role client (monitor.mjs:287,325).
**New:** `fan_spend_events` + rolling-spend view, the decay bands, the morning sweep, the violation ping.

**Effort: 7 days** (5 for the ledger + decay, 2 for the morning sweep; the panel's 5d omitted `/transactions` pagination).

**Causal chain:** one $3k/month whale held for the doc's stated year is $36,000. The system today is structurally blind to a whale *going quiet* — the exact moment intervention is worth money. The morning miss list catches neglect on day one; the decay alert catches lapse at day 10 instead of at the next monthly review. It also retires a $250-lifetime definition of "whale" in favour of the doc's $3k/month one, redirecting expensive bespoke handling from ~420 list members to the ~dozen fans actually spending at that level.

**The one thing that kills it:** alert fatigue. 420 list-whales across decay bands will spam the QA channel and get muted inside a week. **Gate every alert to the computed $3k/30d cohort plus tier A/B accounts only.** Second-order: `whales.cardByAccount` currently holds handling tags for **2 fans out of 420**, so the DO-NOT-SELL ping will almost never fire until the team populates the OF card lists — ship it, but don't sell it as a headline. Third: the git copy of `lastspend.json` is a month stale (newest entry 2026-07-05, today is 2026-08-06). **Pull the live Railway volume copy before deploying any change to the writer** or the backfill seeds from a month-old snapshot.

### T1-C. PPV Unlock → Aftercare Nudge (candidate 0)

**Panel agreement:** Feasibility 8, ROI 7, Duplication 8. Upgraded above its own self-assessment by two lenses.

**Feature list**
1. Each cycle, filter the transactions `sweepTransactions()` already pulls to `type === 'message'` (a PPV unlock, normalised from `descriptionDetails.type`, of.mjs:234-254) inside the last N minutes.
2. Look up that fan's thread in the `/chats` page already fetched; test whether the account sent a message *after* the unlock timestamp (`recentReplies()`, of.mjs:221, already scans exactly this).
3. After a 10-minute grace window with no reply, post **one** nudge into the shift channel — *"@chatter — {fan} just unlocked ${amount} on {account}, no reply in 12 min"* — idempotent per transaction id via `claim()`.
4. Persist every unlock to `ppv_followups` (txn id, account, fan, amount, unlocked_at, first_followup_at, followed_up) → per-chatter **aftercare compliance %** per shift.
5. Guardrails in v1, not v2: per-shift nudge cap, and a value floor (unlocks ≥ $20 only — the normaliser already filters `amount > 0`).
6. **Suppression rule:** skip any thread whose last message changed between cycles (see failure mode).

**Reuses:** `sweepTransactions()` and its already-resolved chatter/QA-on-shift, `recentReplies()`, `claim()`, `postQaPins()`/shift-channel routing, `ACCOUNT_META` tiers for the value floor. **No new poller, no new OF calls.**
**New:** the unlock→reply pairing, the grace timer, `ppv_followups`, the cap/floor.

**Effort: 4 days.**

**Causal chain:** p8 lists follow-ups after PPV *sent* (#2) and after PPV *unlocked* (#3) as two of twenty quality standards, and p9 component 3 asserts the mechanism directly — aftercare "determines the rate of commission you're able to extract per unit of traffic. ($3k consistent shifts)". This is the **only build in the entire set that changes a chatter's behaviour inside the shift the revenue is being lost in.** Everything else is a report someone reads later. Secondarily, aftercare compliance % is a per-chatter metric causally closer to money than response time is.

**Panel correction to carry forward:** the candidate's own stated risk — "message-level sender attribution is broken" — **does not apply to this build.** That defect is confined to the browser client `src/lib/of-api.ts`; this build reads the unlock from `/transactions` and the reply from `/chats` lastMessage via `fromUser.id === fan.id` (of.mjs:70-74), which has been correct all along. Don't let the caveat scare anyone off.

**The one thing that kills it:** `/chats` returns only the *last* message per thread. A chatter who replies and then receives another fan message inside the 240s poll window registers as "no reply" and gets @-mentioned **by name**. A named-person alert that is wrong twice gets muted regardless of the cap. Ship the suppression rule (fire only where the thread's last message is still the fan's), and label the compliance % as approximate.

### T1-D. Monitor Truth Layer — coverage, alert persistence, tier-in-DB, $10k floor (merges candidates 19, the first half of 20, and 4)

**Panel agreement:** [19] scored 9/6/8, [20]-persistence 7/5/7, [4] 5/9/6. Two lenses independently said these three ship together because [20]'s persistence is [4]'s stated prerequisite and [19] protects the tier data [4] puts in the DB.

**Feature list**
1. **Coverage watchdog** *(3d)* — diff the authenticated account set each cycle against the previous cycle and against `ACCOUNT_META`'s expected keys; alert the first time an account disappears, with how long it's been gone. Flag **username drift**: `ACCOUNT_META` and `ACCOUNT_SHEET_ALIASES` are keyed on the *current* OF username, and `tierFor()`/`level2Eligible()` silently default an unknown username to tier C **with no level-2 escalation** — so a rename (June went `thisisjunee` → `junehaynes`) downgrades a tier-A account's protection with no error anywhere. Key on `accountId`, not username. Surface a persistent roster `∅` (monitor.mjs:738 already logs it to a console nobody reads). Add a dead-account check (zero transactions / zero posts / zero inbound for N days). Ships as one consolidated block inside the existing EOD admin post — a green *"coverage: 8/8 accounts, all resolved"* line, so its silence is the reassurance.
2. **Alert persistence** *(2d, not 6)* — write every fired L1/L2/L3 into `downtime_alerts`. Every field the table wants (`of_account_id, fan_id, fan_message_at, level, waited_seconds, chatter_id, qa_chatter_id`) is already computed in-process by `scan()`/`unansweredThreads()` and discarded; `claim()` already provides the idempotency its UNIQUE key expects. **Zero new OF calls.** Fire-and-forget with try/catch — the monitor is deliberately file-state-only for resilience and the OF API already took it down for 11.5 hours once. Four separate candidates proposed this write independently; build it **once, here**.
3. **Tier in the database** *(2d)* — move tier off the hardcoded 8-entry `ACCOUNT_META` map onto the account record, read at boot with a cache and a **fallback to config.mjs** so a Supabase outage can never stop the loop. Manual-override column mandatory.
4. **$10,000 floor alert** *(2d, gated on Blocker B)* — every account whose trailing-30-day revenue is under the p12 guarantee, with the dollar gap and the assigned chatter named, in the existing 08:00 digest.
5. **QA worklist** *(2d)* — one ranked queue: breach count × tier weight × revenue-vs-baseline × whale count, posted at shift start into the QA's channel. This is p7 #5's explicit ask ("which chatter, which team, which account requires your focus… allows us to assure quality at scale"), and it's currently intuition.

**Reuses:** `listAccounts()` (of.mjs:96-107, already parses `is_authenticated`), the `needAuth` array computed at monitor.mjs:724 and thrown away, the roster diagnostic loop at :738, `EOD.adminWebhook`, `claim()`, the `downtime_alerts` migration file, `ACCOUNT_META` as seed + fallback, `api/discord-digest.js` for the 08:00 post.
**New:** `coverage_snapshots`, the tier column + monthly recompute, the floor query, the ranked worklist.

**Effort: 9 days** for the bundle (with the earnings cron from Blocker B counted separately).

**Causal chain:** the $10,000 floor is written as a **guarantee** (p12) and nothing checks it — an account can sit at $4k for a quarter unnoticed, and putting the dollar gap in front of someone every morning forces one of three revenue moves (more chatter focus, more promo slots, drop the account). The coverage half is loss prevention on the largest single loss shape available: a tier-A/B account that de-auths stops being response-time monitored entirely and nobody is told.

**The live leak worth quoting to whoever approves this:** `lillyylou` (Antonella) and `ellaajanee` (Ella) are hardcoded **tier C** in config.mjs right now, which means **no 10-minute level-2 escalation** — on two of the three accounts p25 singles out for attention and two of the five on the p5 swap list. That is a production under-protection today. **It is also a 30-minute manual audit of an 8-entry map — do it in week 1, before the migration.**

**The one thing that kills it:** the panel is right that the doc contradicts itself on B-tier ($10k on p12 vs $20-50k on p7) — **but I'm overruling the claim that this blocks the build.** The p12 **floor** is unambiguous and stated as a guarantee; what conflicts is what *B-tier membership* means. Ship the floor alert at $10k with no human input required; only the tier-derivation thresholds need a decision, and the manual-override column covers the interim. What *would* kill it: revenue-derived tiering reclassifying accounts the team thinks of as B-tier, with no override — it gets switched off in week two.
