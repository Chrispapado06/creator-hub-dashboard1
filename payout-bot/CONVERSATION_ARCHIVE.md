## What payout-bot is

payout-bot is a small standalone Node.js bot (ESM, `.mjs` files, no framework) that lives in this repo alongside the main dashboard app but runs independently, on a fleet of GitHub Actions cron workflows. It exists to answer one operational question for the UNCVRD agency — "what's happening with our creators' OnlyFans money and traffic, right now, without anyone having to log into OF" — and a second, later-bolted-on one about Reddit posters' performance and pay. It talks to the OnlyFansAPI (`app.onlyfansapi.com`) as its data source and posts everything to Telegram and Discord, since that's where the team actually lives day to day.

Concretely, today's `payout-bot/` directory contains:

- **`config.mjs`** — the single source of truth for which creators are tracked, their agency split (`agency_pct`), their payout mode, and their page type (free/paid, which sets the LTV alert threshold). Also holds the shared utilities every other script imports: Discord/Telegram senders (including PDF-attachment variants), timezone-safe date math for UK wall-clock report windows, and a platform-name normalizer used for traffic-source attribution.
- **`bot.mjs`** — polls `GET /{acct}/payouts/payout-requests` every 10 minutes for every creator in `"on_payout_request"` mode and fires a Telegram alert (with the computed agency cut) the moment a new payout request appears. Silently bootstraps each creator's existing history on first sight so it doesn't spam old payouts. State lives in `state.json`, committed back to the repo by the workflow.
- **`weekly.mjs`** — every Monday at 09:00 UTC, computes the prior Mon→Sun window in UK time for creators in `"weekly_net_messages_tips"` mode (currently just Blue Bear), sums net messages + tips from OF's own stats endpoint, applies her 28% agency cut, and posts an invoice-ready breakdown to Telegram for slash.com.
- **`daily.mjs`** — an 08:00 UTC daily traffic report: new+renewing subs, sales, LTV, and a revenue-by-platform-source breakdown per creator, with spike/red-zone flags.
- **`telegram-bot.mjs`** — handles the `/update` (today so far) and `/24` (rolling 24h) on-demand commands in the Telegram group; originally polled `getUpdates` every 5 minutes, now superseded for live use by a Supabase edge function webhook (see "Current status" below) but kept as a manual fallback.
- **`pdf-report.mjs`** — generates the branded PDF reports (UNCVRD wordmark, per-creator tables, revenue charts) attached to the daily report, `/update`/`/24` replies, and the Reddit leaderboard/weekly posts.
- **`reddit-daily.mjs` / `reddit-weekly.mjs` / `reddit-watcher.mjs` / `reddit-leaderboard.mjs` / `reddit-lib.mjs` / `reddit-state.json`** — a second bolted-on subsystem tracking the agency's Reddit promo accounts: daily post/upvote/comment rollups, a 15-minute shadowban/viral watcher, weekly subreddit-ROI rankings, and a poster leaderboard that computes a weekly cash bonus per Reddit poster.
- **`discord-commands/register.mjs`** — a one-time Discord slash-command registration script. It's physically in payout-bot but is actually shared infrastructure: it registers commands for the separate "Bernard" Discord bot (a Supabase Edge Function, `supabase/functions/discord-bot/index.ts`) that handles the VA shift-approval/payroll system and, later, the Shift Downtime Monitor's `/whale` commands. It sits here purely by convention/reuse, not because those bots' logic lives in payout-bot.

## Why it was built

The throughline across every commit message is the same complaint, restated in different forms: numbers the bot reported kept silently disagreeing with what the OF web UI showed the agency owner (Luca), because the bot was computing things itself (summing `/transactions` rows, using UTC boundaries, using gross revenue) instead of asking OF's own stats endpoints for the same numbers the UI renders. A lot of the early history is that discrepancy getting found and fixed one metric at a time:

- **Weekly invoice math** (`0f34ed3`, 2026-05-15): the bot was summing all `/transactions` rows for Blue Bear's weekly net, and it drifted from Luca's own screenshot calculator because of status-filtering, refund-handling, and timezone-bucketing differences. Switched to calling `/statistics/statements/earnings` (type=messages, type=tips) directly — the same data source the OF UI itself uses — and it matched Luca's number to the cent ($18,865.34 net → $5,282.30 at 28%).
- **Report windows in UK time, not UTC** (`bd42b58`, 2026-05-15): daily and weekly boundaries are now computed in `Europe/London` wall time (DST-aware via `Intl.DateTimeFormat`, not a hardcoded offset) because that's the timezone the agency actually works in and the OF UI's day boundaries follow.
- **LTV numerator switched to net, not gross** (`11dbc0e`, 2026-05-19): Luca's mental model of "LTV = Rev / new subs" meant net (post-OF-20%-cut) revenue, not gross — gross was inflating every creator's LTV by roughly 25% and didn't match what the agency saw on its own books.
- **LTV switched to lifetime instead of day-over-day** (`249e9fc`, 2026-05-15): a single day's ratio was too noisy to be a useful red-zone flag; lifetime gross ÷ unique new subscribers is stable and is what now drives the red-zone alert.
- **Sub counts fixed for FTL creators** (`3469646`, 2026-05-24): Marissa runs a Free Trial Link, so most of her sign-ups are free promo sign-ups that never touch `/transactions` — the bot was reporting ~30 subs/day when the real number was 200+. Fixed by pulling subs from `/statistics/subscriber-metrics` (pro-rated across the 1–2 calendar days a rolling window overlaps) while keeping `/transactions` for the dollar figures, which need hour-level precision the stats endpoints don't offer.
- **Traffic-source attribution** (`2a7cfb7`, `a5482eb`, 2026-05-27): building the "By source" (Instagram/Reddit/TikTok/etc.) breakdown surfaced two more gotchas — OF has *two different* endpoints for trial links vs. campaign "tracking links," and both endpoints' summary/lifetime fields ignore the date range you pass them; the actually-windowed numbers live in a `daily_metrics` array that has to be summed by hand.

Alongside the OF-accuracy work, there's a parallel thread of just making the reports pleasant to read in Telegram/Discord without hitting platform limits — combining nine separate per-creator messages into one (`676f633`), splitting Reddit's daily report back out into multiple messages when it blew past Discord's 2,000-character webhook cap (`3420f10`), switching from Discord embeds to a plain-text "Chatter Points Bot" house style the agency preferred (`90bfb98`), and eventually attaching a branded PDF to every summary message instead of just plain text (`44d990d`).

## Key decisions and tradeoffs

- **Agency splits are per-creator, not global.** `config.mjs` defaults new creators to a 50/50 split and `on_payout_request` real-time alerting, but Blue Bear is a hand-tuned exception: 28% agency cut, computed weekly off net messages+tips only (subscriptions are deliberately excluded from her invoicing). The default split moved around early on — Marissa was explicitly set to 50/50 (`3b7b74c`), then the whole roster's default was set to 50/50 while carving out Blue Bear's 72/28 as the one exception (`d8834a7`).
- **Two payout modes, on purpose.** `"on_payout_request"` (real-time Telegram ping the moment a creator requests a payout) vs. `"weekly_net_messages_tips"` (Monday invoice math) are intentionally separate code paths (`bot.mjs` vs `weekly.mjs`) sharing only `config.mjs` — creators on the weekly mode are explicitly skipped by the real-time poller so the two never double-report.
- **Page type (free/paid) drives the LTV alert threshold**, not a fixed number: paid pages flag under $30 LTV, free pages under $5. Several creators got reclassified over time as their actual monetization model became clear (Meg/Emma/Marissa → paid via FTL; June → free) rather than by assumption.
- **PDF reports are skipped on transactional alerts.** Payout-request, shadowban, and viral alerts stay plain-text/one-line by design — a PDF adds no value to a single event, only to a summary.
- **Reddit poster pay formula churned a lot before it settled** (roughly a dozen tuning commits on 2026-05-22 alone): it moved from a per-post + per-upvote + per-comment + viral-bonus model, through a shared weekly pool cap, to its final shape — a pure "$1 per 1,000 upvotes (capped at 2,000/post) × account-tier multiplier, minus a flat $0.10 per removed post, capped at $50/poster/week." An OF-revenue-attribution component (0.5% of net revenue split proportionally across posters) was added then deliberately removed again in favor of "upvotes only," since the agency wanted a pure performance signal Reddit posters could see and predict.
- **Account tiers reward effort on small/new accounts.** A karma-based multiplier (warm-up ×3 down to mature ×1) applies to positive earnings so a poster growing a brand-new subreddit account isn't structurally out-earned by someone posting on an already-huge one — but the multiplier deliberately does *not* apply to the removed-post penalty, since small accounts already draw more moderator scrutiny and shouldn't be double-punished for it.
- **`discord-commands/register.mjs` living in payout-bot is a documented architectural quirk, not a design choice for payout-bot itself.** The actual VA shift-approval/payroll bot logic and (later) the whale-tracking `/whale` command both live in a separate Supabase Edge Function ("Bernard," `supabase/functions/discord-bot/index.ts`), because a Deno edge function can't read files out of this repo. The registration script stayed in payout-bot purely because it was the first place a "register a slash command" utility existed, and every later Discord-bot feature just added its command definitions to the same file rather than build a second one.
- **`/update` and `/24` were eventually moved off cron polling** onto a real-time Supabase edge function (`telegram-webhook`) that Telegram pushes messages to directly, because polling `getUpdates` every 2–5 minutes was slower and noisier than it needed to be. `telegram-bot.mjs` and its GitHub Actions workflow were kept, but the workflow's schedule trigger was removed — it's `workflow_dispatch`-only now, there as a manual fallback if the webhook is ever disabled.

## Bugs and gotchas hit along the way

- OF's `/trial-links` and `/tracking-links` summary fields (`subs_total`, `revenue_total`, etc.) are **lifetime totals regardless of the date range you pass** — the actually date-scoped numbers live in a per-day `daily_metrics` array that has to be summed client-side. Missing this would have made every "By source" report show lifetime numbers mislabeled as "yesterday's."
- Reddit **IP-blocks Supabase Edge Function egress**, which broke the shift-approval bot's attempt to cross-check posters' claimed shift activity directly against Reddit. The workaround was a separate GitHub Actions cron (`reddit-poll.mjs`, every 5 minutes) that polls each account's RSS feed from a normal GitHub-hosted runner and upserts into a `reddit_posts` Supabase table, which the Discord bot then reads from instead of hitting Reddit itself.
- Discord's webhook content cap (2,000 characters) and embed cap (6,000 characters) were both hit and fixed reactively — the Reddit daily report initially crammed all creators into one message and started failing with HTTP 400 once there were enough creators; it now sends one header + one message per creator + one footer, matching the pattern already used by the weekly report.
- pdf-lib's stock Helvetica font throws on non-WinAnsi characters (arrows, emoji) — `pdf-report.mjs` sanitizes report text before drawing to avoid PDF generation crashing on something as simple as a 🚀 emoji in a flag label.
- Clock-in/out times for the shift-approval bot needed explicit Philippine-time handling (`dfb0614`) since the whole poster roster works PHT evenings; UTC-stored, PHT-rendered, with manual next-day rollover handling for a shift that crosses midnight.
- Bella Leah has an OF API connection that was never authenticated — she's deliberately left out of `config.mjs`'s `CREATORS` list (with a comment explaining why) rather than added and left to 404 on every single fetch. This is the one clearly-flagged "known gap, waiting on an external fix" item in the codebase itself.

The `telegram-bot.yml` workflow's schedule was deliberately disabled in favor of the Supabase webhook, as described above, and is dispatch-only now.

`payout-bot/state.json` (the payout-request dedup ledger) was last updated 2026-07-15, matching the last "bot: update state.json" commit in the repo's history — there's been no new payout-request activity logged since then even though the workflow keeps running every 10 minutes on schedule (a commit only lands when the state actually changes). That's most likely just "no creator has requested a payout in the last two weeks" rather than a broken bot, but it's worth a quick sanity check against the live OF connections next time someone touches this project, given other bots in this repo (notably Shift Downtime Monitor) have separately documented OF accounts flickering in and out of authentication.

payout-bot also ended up as the reference implementation other bots in this repo were explicitly told to copy: the Shift Downtime Monitor project's own memory notes describe cloning "the payout-bot pattern" — GitHub Actions cron + a committed `state.json` ledger + a shared `config.mjs` of small helpers (`sendDiscord`, timezone utilities, a `CREATORS`-style roster) — as its starting architecture.
