# UNCVRD Ad Tracker — quick look

Thanks for taking a look! This tracks ad spend (Meta + OnlyFinder) against
OnlyFans revenue per split-test variant, and tells you what to **scale**, **keep**
or **cut**.

## See it in 10 seconds
- Double-click **`dashboard.html`** → it opens in your browser with sample data.
  (Needs internet for the charts.) Nothing to install.
- Play with the **SCALE ≥** / **CUT <** boxes top-right to see the calls change.

## What you're looking at
- **KPI cards** — total spend, revenue, profit, blended ROAS, new fans, LTV.
- **Today's calls** — auto-ranked "scale this / cut that" + a budget-move tip.
- **Profit vs salary** — per creator, is the ad profit covering their salary?
- **Variant leaderboard** — every test ranked, with a SCALE/KEEP/CUT badge.
- **Platform summary** — Meta vs OnlyFinder.

## The spreadsheet
- **`UNCVRD_Ad_Tracker.xlsx`** is where data gets entered each day (one row per
  ad/variant). Open in Excel or import to Google Sheets.
- The dashboard is just a **view** on that data — the numbers match the sheet.

## Telling us what to change
Easiest: leave comments on the shared Google Sheet, or just jot notes on:
- metrics or columns you'd add
- the SCALE/KEEP/CUT logic
- anything on the dashboard you'd lay out differently
